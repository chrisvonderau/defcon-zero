HOW TO PLAY

HELIX, a rogue AI, is forging missile alerts between nations. Every fake attack pushes the world toward war. If the DEFCON clock reaches 1, it is over.

War Room: pick a glowing flashpoint and jack in. Win the hack to calm that region. While you work, launch drone swarms and deploy AI agents. They run on their own and then cool down.

Hacks rotate: FIREWALL BREACH (twin-stick shooter), SENTRY MAZE (grab the keys, reach the exit), CIPHER LOCK (crack the code before the trace finds you). Your AI companions fight alongside you. Fill AI OVERCLOCK and unleash it.

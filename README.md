# DEFCON Zero

A rogue AI called HELIX is forging missile alerts to start World War III. You are ZERO, a hacker with your own AI crew: ECHO, plus drone swarms and field agents.

- **War Room:** a retro green vector world map with a DEFCON clock. Launch Recon, Interceptor, Jammer, and EMP drone swarms. Deploy Scout, Defender, Negotiator, and Decoy AI agents.
- **Hacks:** neon cyberspace levels that rotate between Firewall Breach (twin-stick shooter), Sentry Maze, and Cipher Lock. AI companions fight alongside you, and AI Overclock gives you a burst of power.
- **Modes:** Solo, 2-player co-op, and 2-player versus campaigns, plus Endless Defense (solo or co-op) with its own high-score table. Optional AI hackers can join either side. Difficulties are Easy, Normal, and Hard, plus Doomsday, which unlocks when you beat the campaign.
- **Mod chips:** equippable upgrades bought with credits. They change how drones and agents behave or add an active ability (Surge, Blackout).
- **Controls:** Xbox controller (standard mapping, up to 2 pads, rumble), remappable keyboard and mouse, or iPad touch (auto-aim or Fire-button mode).
- **One offline HTML file.** No network requests, no tracking, and no ads. Saves stay in your browser.

Play: https://chrisvonderau.github.io/defcon-zero/
